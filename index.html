Questions from partners

What a firm will want settled before launch

Does it give legal advice?

No, it never does. When a caller asks what they should do, it takes their details and tells them someone from the firm will respond.

Who is responsible for what it says?

The firm remains responsible. The script is agreed with you before launch, later changes go through you as well, and checking it against your professional duties is a job for the firm.

How is confidentiality handled?

An agent is given only what the scoped task requires, and summaries go only to the people you name. Where data is held, how long it is kept and which software it connects to are all confirmed and written into your scope before anything goes live.

Can it run conflict checks?

No. It collects the details and your team runs that check.

Is it different from a legal answering service?

A legal answering service or law firm answering service generally takes a message. An agent can be scoped to act on a schedule too, such as confirming appointments or chasing documents. For front-desk cover on its own, see our sister site on answering calls for solicitors.

Can it deal with new enquiries?

Some firms call this AI legal intake. An agent can be scoped to note the basic facts and pass them on, but it never decides whether the firm takes the matter.

Is a caller told they are speaking to software?

Yes. The agent tells them early in the call, using wording the firm has approved.
